FAQ

Frequently

questions

Can’t find it here? Create a free account and ask the AI coach anything about your trading.

Can TRADElogs place or close trades on my account?
No. You connect with MT5’s investor (read-only) password, which can view your history but cannot open, modify or close positions.
Which brokers work?
Any broker that offers MetaTrader 5. You only need your MT5 login, investor password and your broker’s server name.
How much does it cost?
TRADElogs is free while we’re getting started, and you don’t need a card to sign up.
What happens to my password and data?
Your investor password is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) so we can keep syncing, and it can only read your account. Disconnecting a broker from the Brokers page deletes its synced data.
Do I have to import trades by hand?
Never. Once your account is connected, your history backfills automatically and new trades appear as they close.
